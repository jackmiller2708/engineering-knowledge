# Experiment — <title>

Headings follow `KNOWLEDGE_STANDARD.md` section 7. Keep experiments small. Keep failed experiments when they teach something.

## Objective
What question is this experiment meant to answer?

## Hypothesis
What behavior is expected, and why? (Write this before running.)

## Setup
Environment, tool versions, implementation, assumptions. No machine-specific paths.

## Procedure
Steps another person can repeat.

## Observations
What actually happened. Raw output, not interpretation.

## Analysis
How the observations relate to the hypothesis.

## Conclusion
What has been demonstrated, and what remains unproven. Sources for any documented claim.
